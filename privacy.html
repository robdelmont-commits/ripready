Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

RipReady ("we," "our") operates ripready.app and the RipReady Chrome extension, a browser-based live streaming tool for Whatnot sellers.

What We Collect

The RipReady Chrome extension detects a single, specific local connection attempt made by Whatnot's own website (a WebSocket request to your local machine, standard OBS Studio behavior) and relays the resulting live-stream authorization token to RipReady's backend. This token is used solely to establish your live stream session through the RipReady studio.

What We Don't Collect

The extension does not read, log, or transmit your browsing history, personal files, passwords, or any content on websites other than whatnot.com. It does not access camera or microphone data beyond what's needed to run your own live stream, which you initiate directly in your browser.

Where Data Goes

The stream authorization token is passed through our relay server (Railway) and stored temporarily in our database (Supabase) solely to activate your live stream. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, and is not used for any purpose beyond enabling your Whatnot broadcast.

Data Retention

Tokens are per-broadcast and are replaced or discarded on each new session.
