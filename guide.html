RipReady Setup

Go live in
five minutes.

No OBS, no config files, no hardware box under your desk. Just a browser tab and a camera.

5 steps · under 5 minutes
1

Install the extension

This is what lets RipReady talk to Whatnot. One click, no setup screen.

chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ripready
RipReady — Whatnot Stream Bridge
Connects your Whatnot show to RipReady. No OBS required.
Add to Chrome
2

Open RipReady, turn on your camera

Go to ripready.app/studio and click Camera. Allow access when Chrome asks.

No token — waiting for RipReady extension
3

Put RipReady and Whatnot in separate windows

Side by side on one screen is fine. Just make sure they're two windows, not two tabs in the same window.

ripready.app/studio
RipReady Studio
Camera live, waiting to connect
whatnot.com
Your Show
Stream via OBS Tools
!

Why this matters: Chrome pauses tabs sitting in the background, which can freeze your stream mid-broadcast. Two separate windows avoids this completely. You don't need two monitors, just snap or tile both windows on the one you have.

4

Connect on Whatnot

In your Whatnot show, this is the exact path:

→

You don't need to do anything else here. RipReady picks this up automatically and connects itself in the background.

5

You're live

Switch back to the RipReady window. It goes live on its own, no button to click.
